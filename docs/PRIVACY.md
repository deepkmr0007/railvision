# Privacy notice & consent (draft for review)

**What we store**
* Tracking tasks you create (train, date, stations, interval, destinations).
* For WhatsApp: your phone number (needed to deliver messages), whether you opted in, and the time of your last message (to respect WhatsApp's 24-hour window). Message bodies are not stored.
* Consent history as a **keyed hash** of your number with the action (opt-in / opt-out) and time.
* Notifications sent to you and the delivery result per channel.

**What we never ask for or store**: IRCTC passwords, booking credentials, PNR passenger details, payment data.

**Your controls**
* WhatsApp: reply **STOP** to stop updates, **START** to resume, **DELETE MY DATA** to remove your number and link.
* Dashboard: pause, stop or delete any task at any time.

**Consent rules we follow**: we only message people who wrote to us first or linked their account with a one-time code. We never message purchased or scraped numbers. Business-initiated WhatsApp updates use Meta-approved templates.

**Data accuracy**: live information is shown with its source and timestamp. Estimates are always labelled. Chart vacancy is not a booking guarantee.

**Retention (recommended production settings)**: observations 30 days, notifications 90 days, delivery attempts 90 days, audit log 1 year, consent records as long as required by law.
